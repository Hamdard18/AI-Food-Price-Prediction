# AI-Based Food Price Prediction in Afghanistan

**Building AI course project**

## Summary

This project explores how Artificial Intelligence can analyze historical food prices in Afghanistan and predict possible price changes. It aims to help families, farmers, and small businesses make better economic decisions.

## Background

Food prices have a direct impact on people's daily lives in Afghanistan. Many households face economic difficulties when the prices of essential products such as wheat, flour, rice, cooking oil, and sugar increase.

Food prices can change because of several factors:

- Changes in supply and demand.
- Transportation costs and fuel prices.
- Weather conditions and drought.
- Import restrictions and international market changes.
- Economic instability and currency fluctuations.

The motivation behind this project is to explore how AI can help people understand food price trends and prepare for possible price increases.

## How is it used?

The proposed system would collect historical food price information from different markets and cities in Afghanistan.

Users would select a food product and a city to view historical prices and estimated future price trends.

Potential users include:

- Families planning their household budgets.
- Farmers and agricultural producers.
- Small shop owners and food retailers.
- Researchers and policymakers.

The system could present its results through a simple website or mobile application, using charts and easy-to-understand predictions.

## Data sources and AI methods

The project would depend on historical food price data from reliable sources, such as:

- [World Food Programme (WFP)](https://www.wfp.org/)
- [FAOSTAT](https://www.fao.org/faostat/)
- [World Bank Data](https://data.worldbank.org/)
- Publicly available Afghan market price reports and statistics.

Potential data would include product names, market locations, dates, and recorded prices.

The proposed AI techniques include:

- **Linear Regression:** To study relationships between food prices and relevant factors.
- **Time Series Analysis:** To identify trends in historical prices and forecast possible future changes.
- **Machine Learning:** To improve predictions using historical market data.

The first version would focus on analyzing historical prices and developing a basic prediction model.

## Challenges

The project has several limitations:

- Reliable and regularly updated data may not be available for every region.
- Some historical records may be incomplete or inconsistent.
- Unexpected events can cause sudden price changes.
- Predictions may be less accurate in areas with limited data.
- The system cannot guarantee future prices.

The predictions would be estimates and should not be treated as guaranteed market information.

## What next?

The project could be developed further by:

- Covering more cities and local markets across Afghanistan.
- Including additional agricultural and food products.
- Developing a mobile application.
- Providing notifications about significant price changes.
- Improving prediction accuracy with more reliable data.
- Collaborating with local universities, researchers, and humanitarian organizations.

In the long term, the project could support better access to market information and help communities make more informed economic decisions.
